1. Purpose & Lawful Basis

All personal data processed inside Marcus AI: Home Launcher remains strictly on your device to perform the launcher, voice assistant, Direct LLM Chat, note-taking, alarm, media playback, and HUD Gallery functions you directly invoke (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(b) — performance of a requested service, and Article 6(1)(a) — explicit consent for optional camera face unlock, GPS weather, notification access, and voice memory logging).

3. Zero-PII Filtering in the Offline Knowledge Bank

Before any public factual entry, Direct Chat web snippet summary, or plugin-contributed knowledge fact is written to knowledge_bank.json, KnowledgeBankManager strips any https?:// URLs and @handle mentions and runs a strict two-stage check:

  • Deterministic Regex Blocker: Rejects entries containing your configured user name, email addresses, phone numbers, street addresses, UK postcodes, IPv4 addresses, API keys/tokens (such as AIza..., hf_..., sk-..., ghp_..., Bearer), 6+ consecutive digits, or personal query indicators (my name is, my address, my phone, my password, my bank, my national insurance, my social security, etc.).
  • On-Device LLM Privacy Auditor: When a local model is loaded, evaluates sanitized topics and summaries via a strict UK GDPR prompt and rejects anything not explicitly returned as SAFE.

4. Your UK GDPR Rights Built Into Settings

Because your data stays on your device, you exercise your data protection rights instantaneously in Settings → Privacy, Security & Legal and the Marcus Hub Memory view:

  • Right of Access & Portability: View all stored conversation turns, intents, and knowledge entries in-app, and export memory.csv, intents.json, or knowledge_bank.json anytime.
  • Right to Rectification & Selective Erasure: Edit or delete any individual memory turn, Direct Chat session, Private Folder or imported Gallery item, note, face profile, intent, voice recording, or knowledge entry directly in-app.
  • Right to Restrict Processing: Toggle Record memory.csv Conversation Log off at any time, or keep 100% Offline Privacy Mode ON.
  • Right to Erasure (“Right to Be Forgotten”): Tap Erase All Data in Settings → Privacy, Security & Legal (or Marcus Memory Control) to permanently wipe conversation logs (memory.csv / sync_memory.csv), Cerebellum Markov habit sequences, Lill-E emotional state, knowledge_bank.json, custom plugins, Direct Chat sessions, Private Gallery Vault files & salted PIN, Voice Vault M4A recordings, notes, alarms, reminders, custom wallpaper, Hugging Face token, auto-read contacts, captured notification/message alerts, and enrolled face profiles in a single local operation.
