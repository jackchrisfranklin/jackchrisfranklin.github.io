3. Zero-PII Knowledge Bank Safeguards

Whenever a public factual summary from Direct Chat web search or a user plugin is proposed for caching in knowledge_bank.json, KnowledgeBankManager strips URLs and @handle mentions and runs two local checks before saving:

  • Deterministic Regex Blocker: Rejects candidates containing your configured user name, email addresses, telephone numbers, street addresses, UK postcodes, IPv4 addresses, API keys or bearer tokens (such as AIza..., hf_..., sk-..., ghp_...), 6+ consecutive digits, or personal query indicators (such as “my name is”, “my address”, “my national insurance”, “my bank”, or “my password”).
  • Local Privacy Auditor: When a local LLM is loaded in memory, a prompt checks the candidate topic and summary and discards any entry that is not explicitly evaluated as SAFE.

4. Exercising Your UK GDPR Rights Inside the App

You can exercise your rights directly in Settings → Privacy, Security & Legal without writing to us:

  • Right of Access & Data Portability (Articles 15 & 20): Inspect stored turns, custom intents, and cached facts in-app, and export memory.csv, intents.json, or knowledge_bank.json at any time.
  • Right to Rectification & Selective Deletion (Articles 16 & 17): Edit or delete individual conversation turns, Direct Chat sessions, notes, Private Folder or imported Gallery items, Voice Vault recordings, face profiles, plugins, or Knowledge Bank entries.
  • Right to Restrict Processing (Article 18): Turn off Record memory.csv Conversation Log, keep 100% Offline Privacy Mode ON, or disable optional Notification Listener / GPS / Camera access.
  • One-Tap Complete Erasure (“Right to Be Forgotten”, Article 17): Tapping Erase All Personal Data in Settings → Privacy, Security & Legal permanently deletes memory.csv / sync_memory.csv, Cerebellum Markov sequences, Lill-E emotional state, knowledge_bank.json, custom plugins, Direct Chat sessions, Private Gallery Vault files and salted PIN, Voice Vault .m4a recordings, notes, alarms, reminders, custom wallpaper (and restores the default Dark Grid backdrop), Hugging Face token, auto-read contacts, captured notifications, and enrolled face profiles. Publicly licensed open AI/speech model weights (files/models/, files/piper_voices/, files/offline_stt/) contain no personal data and may be deleted separately inside AI or Voice Settings, or removed altogether by uninstalling the app.
