At a Glance

  • No user accounts or developer servers: You do not create an account to use Marcus AI: Home Launcher, and we do not operate a server that stores your messages, voice audio, installed apps, photos, notes, or location.
  • Zero advertising or analytics SDKs: The application includes no advertising networks, behavioural profiling, or automatic background crash telemetry.
  • Offline by default: 100% Offline Privacy Mode is switched ON when first installed. Outbound weather, Direct Chat web searches, and optional AI/voice downloads occur only if you choose to enable Online Mode or initiate a Google Play purchase.
  • Local control and full erasure: Private files are excluded from Google Cloud Auto Backup, and one-tap Erase All Personal Data wipes your conversation logs, Private Gallery Vault, Voice Vault recordings, notes, alarms, tokens, and face profiles from the device.
