Android Permissions & System Capabilities Disclosure (v0.2.39)

Exact breakdown matching every declared permission, optional hardware feature, foreground service, intent query, and Picture-in-Picture / lockscreen integration in AndroidManifest.xml (Build 59, com.jchristopherfranklin.marcus).

Permissions Intentionally Omitted for Privacy

Marcus AI: Home Launcher avoids sensitive inbox, call-log, and background tracking permissions:

  • READ_SMS, RECEIVE_SMS & READ_CALL_LOG — Not declared (optional notification listener covers allowlisted alerts strictly in memory).
  • ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION — Not declared (optional weather GPS only updates while the app is open and Offline Mode is disabled).
  • BLUETOOTH_SCAN declares android:usesPermissionFlags="neverForLocation" — used strictly to find audio accessories, never for geolocation.
  • All hardware sensors (camera.front, camera.flash) declare android:required="false" so tablets and devices without them remain fully supported.

Permission / Declaration Scope Exact Feature Justification

REQUEST_DELETE_PACKAGES		 Core Home Launcher (CATEGORY_HOME) Essential for any Android Home Screen replacement: enumerates installed apps in the App Drawer, launches them, supports long-press Hide/Disable/App-Info, and invokes Android’s OS uninstall confirmation dialog when requested by the user. Manifest <queries> intents Targeted OS hand-off Declares visibility to Android’s built-in launcher apps, Bluetooth enable/settings, SpeechRecognizer & TTS engines, Clock/Timer/Camera apps, default HTTPS browser (WEB_SEARCH / VIEW), image/video viewers for System Gallery hand-off, and email client (mailto:) for anonymous bug reports.
