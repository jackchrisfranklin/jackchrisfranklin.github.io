Privacy Policy

App: Marcus AI: Home Launcher (internal codename Marcus-2, release v0.2.4 / Marcus Beta 4, Build 28)
Android Package: com.jchristopherfranklin.marcus
Developer & Data Controller: Jack Chris Franklin (jackchrisfranklin@gmail.com)

1. Core Privacy Architecture

Marcus AI: Home Launcher is an Android home-screen replacement and on-device voice assistant designed around local processing. By default, 100% Offline Privacy Mode is enabled at initial launch, blocking background network requests unless you explicitly turn Offline Privacy Mode off or trigger a manual network action.

  • No Developer Servers: We do not operate cloud servers to collect, store, profile, or monetize your personal data.
  • No Ads or Trackers: The app contains zero advertising SDKs, zero third-party analytics, and zero behavioural tracking.
  • No Account Required: You do not create an account, password, or cloud profile to use any feature.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

All app data is stored locally inside your device’s private application storage directory and SharedPreferences:

  • Assistant Preferences: Your configured assistant name, honorific/user name, wallpaper choice, layout order, Piper TTS voice settings, and low-battery alert settings.
  • Notes, Alarms & Reminders: Offline notes, countdown/alarm records, and reminder schedules you create inside the launcher.
  • Conversation & Memory Logs (sync_memory.csv / exported memory.csv): Optional local conversation turns saved only on your device. You can pause logging, edit or delete single entries, export memory.csv, or clear all logs in Settings → Privacy, Security & Legal or the Marcus Hub Memory view.
  • Optional Face Recognition Profiles: Face unlock security is OFF by default. If you enable it and enroll a face via camera, mathematical facial geometry signatures are stored exclusively on-device in private SharedPreferences and never transmitted anywhere.
  • Local Audio Library: Your offline Music Player indexes local audio files via Android MediaStore.Audio or folders you manually select. No photos or videos are scanned or indexed in v0.2.4.
  • Downloaded AI Models & Voices: Optional LiteRT-LM model weights and Piper ONNX voice files reside inside private internal directories (filesDir/models, filesDir/piper_voices).

3. Zero-PII Offline JSON Knowledge Bank

When enabled, Marcus can cache anonymized public summaries from general factual web searches into an on-device file (knowledge_bank.json) so you can retrieve factual answers offline without Wi-Fi.

  • Dual-Layer Privacy Guard: Candidate queries, topics, and summaries are sanitized (stripping https?:// URLs and @handle mentions) and screened before saving by both deterministic regex filters (blocking emails, phone numbers, street addresses, UK postcodes, IPv4 addresses, API keys/tokens, 6+ digit numeric identifiers, your configured user name, and personal phrases like “my address” or “my password”) and the local on-device LLM Privacy Auditor when a model is loaded.
  • Full User Control: Search caching can be paused or wiped with one tap inside Settings → Battery, Weather & Search, or exported as plain JSON.

4. Notification Access & Call Screening (Optional)

If you explicitly grant Android Notification Listener Access inside Android system settings, Marcus reads recent notification headlines locally in memory so it can:

  • Show playback controls for external music players on your Media page.
  • Optionally announce incoming calls and read messages aloud strictly for contacts you explicitly add to your Auto-Read Contacts allowlist.
  • No notifications or contacts are uploaded off your device.

5. Optional Outbound Network Requests

Network connections occur only in these user-initiated or user-enabled scenarios (and are blocked in background whenever Offline Privacy Mode is ON):

  • Weather Updates: Manual city lookup (Open-Meteo / wttr.in) or optional GPS coordinates if you enable Automatic GPS Location Updates. Your location is never sent to our servers because we operate none.
  • General Web Search: When you ask Marcus to search the web while network access is permitted, the query is fetched from DuckDuckGo or Wikipedia (en.wikipedia.org) public APIs/pages.
  • Optional Model & Voice Downloads: Downloading LiteRT-LM models (such as Gemma 4, Gemma 3, or Qwen 3 presets) from Hugging Face (with an optional user-supplied read token stored privately on-device) or Piper ONNX voices from Hugging Face.
  • Optional Anonymous Bug Reports: If an unhandled error triggers the Anti-Crash Monitor or you open Settings → Apps & Support and explicitly tap “Send Anonymous Bug Report”, the app opens your chosen email client addressed to jackchrisfranklin@gmail.com containing an automatically scrubbed report (redacting emails, HTTP/HTTPS URLs, API tokens, IPv4 addresses, filesystem/content:// paths, phone numbers, and quoted strings). Nothing is sent without your explicit send action in your email app.

6. Children’s Privacy

Marcus AI: Home Launcher is a general-utility launcher and assistant not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum digital consent age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
