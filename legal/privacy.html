1. Who We Are and How This App Works

Marcus AI: Home Launcher is an Android home screen replacement (CATEGORY_HOME) and local voice assistant developed and published by Jack Christopher Franklin. For the purposes of the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018, Jack Christopher Franklin is the data controller for any limited processing governed by this policy.

The application is designed so that your launcher layout, assistant state, files, speech, and personal history remain on your own Android device. We do not operate proprietary user accounts, cloud sync servers, advertising SDKs, or behavioural analytics SDKs.

Offline by default: When you first install the app, 100% Offline Privacy Mode is switched ON, which blocks outbound requests for weather forecasts, Direct Chat web searches, and optional AI/voice downloads. Step 1 of Guided Setup explains this setting and invites you to switch Online Mode ON if you want live weather, live web lookup, or model downloads—the choice is always yours.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

When you use Marcus AI: Home Launcher, the following information is stored strictly inside your device’s private application storage and SharedPreferences:

  • Launcher Layout & Appearance: Your configured assistant name, honorific/title, interface mode (Mobile, Desktop, or Adaptive), Home and Bottom Dock shortcut selections, icon size and label preferences, Dark Grid or other geometric backdrop pattern choices, and optional custom wallpaper image (custom_wallpaper.jpg).
  • Notes, Alarms, Timers, Reminders & Voice Vault: Personal notes, schedule items, and optional .m4a audio memos recorded with the Voice Notes Recorder inside files/voice_vault/.
  • Conversation Logs, Direct Chat & Habit Sequences: Optional home conversation turns (sync_memory.csv, exported as memory.csv), standalone Direct LLM Chat sessions (direct_chat_sessions_v1.json), and Markov habit transitions (Cerebellum). Direct LLM Chat queries your offline Knowledge Bank first and reads memory.csv strictly read-only when relevant. Logging can be paused, edited, exported, or wiped at any time.
  • Installed App Routing (App-First Voice Control): Marcus scans installed launcher packages locally via Android’s package manager so you can launch installed or system apps (for example, opening Google Keep when you ask to open Keep Notes) before falling back to Marcus’s own applets unless Marcus is explicitly named. Your installed app list never leaves your phone.
  • HUD Gallery & Private Folder: Indexes local photos, videos, and audio strictly on-device with zero autoplay. Items moved to the Private Folder are copied into private app storage (files/gallery_private/ and gallery_vault_v1.json) and locked with a locally salted SHA-256 PIN hash (and optional local face unlock). The vault locks automatically when you exit the Gallery or turn off the screen. If you forget your Private Folder PIN, you can recover access using Android’s system lockscreen prompt (KeyguardManager): Android verifies your device PIN, pattern, or biometric natively and returns only a pass or fail result to Marcus.
  • Optional Face Recognition Profiles: Off by default. If enabled, mathematical facial geometry vectors are stored only on your device inside SharedPreferences; no face images or vectors are transmitted off the phone.
  • Downloaded AI Models, Voices & Optional Offline STT Pack: Downloaded LiteRT-LM language models (files/models/), Piper-TTS ONNX voices (files/piper_voices/), and the optional Wav2Vec2-Base-960h quantized ONNX speech-to-text backup (files/offline_stt/) live solely in private app storage and are excluded from Google Cloud Auto Backup and device-to-device transfers.

3. Voice Recognition & Custom ROM Offline Backup

For normal voice input, Marcus invokes Android’s system SpeechRecognizer service configured on your device (whose handling depends on your chosen Android system speech provider, such as Google Speech Services).

For devices or custom ROMs that lack a system speech recognition service (or when system recognition fails), Marcus includes an optional offline Wav2Vec2-Base-960h ONNX speech-to-text engine. Nothing is downloaded automatically. Only if you manually tap Download Offline STT (~95 MB) while Online Mode is enabled—or manually import your own model_quantized.onnx and vocab.json files from device storage—will the offline model be installed in Settings → Assistant & Voice. Once installed, voice capture uses Android’s AudioRecord at 16 kHz and transcribes speech completely offline in device memory without sending audio recordings over the network.

4. Knowledge Bank, Direct Chat Web Search & Zero-PII Filtering

When enabled, the local Knowledge Bank (knowledge_bank.json) caches concise, non-personal factual summaries on your device. Both home commands and Direct LLM Chat check your offline Knowledge Bank before searching online. When Online Mode is active and Direct Chat retrieves public search snippets (from DuckDuckGo Instant/HTML and Wikipedia summaries), results are automatically screened and stripped of URLs, @handles, emails, phone numbers, street addresses, UK postcodes, IP addresses, API tokens, 6+ digit numbers, your configured name, and personal phrasing before being saved for offline recall.

5. User-Created Plugins

Zero third-party plugins are pre-installed. If you create or import local scripts (JavaScript, TypeScript, Python, or JSON) under Settings → Plugins & Extensions, those scripts run locally under the capabilities you explicitly grant and respect 100% Offline Privacy Mode.

7. Outbound Network Requests (Only When Online Mode Is Enabled or Triggered)

When 100% Offline Privacy Mode is switched OFF (or when you explicitly confirm a purchase or bug report), outbound network requests occur strictly for:

  • Weather Forecasts: Requests to Open-Meteo or wttr.in for your chosen city or (if opted in) GPS coordinates.
  • Live Web Search in Direct Chat: Queries to DuckDuckGo and Wikipedia summaries when you ask Direct Chat a live or web-search question; launcher web searches hand off directly to your default browser via ACTION_VIEW.
  • User-Initiated Model & Voice Downloads: Downloading LiteRT-LM language models, Piper ONNX voices, or the optional Wav2Vec2 ONNX speech backup from Hugging Face only when you tap Download (using your optional Hugging Face token if supplied for gated downloads).
  • Google Play & RevenueCat Verification: Retrieving prices, verifying purchases, restoring purchases, or opening Customer Center.
  • Manual Anonymous Bug Reports: Triggered only if you tap Send Anonymous Bug Report, which opens your own email app addressed to jackchrisfranklin@gmail.com after scrubbing file paths, email addresses, URLs, IP addresses, phone numbers, and tokens from local diagnostics.
