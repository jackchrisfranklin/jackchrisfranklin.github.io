Quick Answers & Launcher Navigation

  • Why don’t live Weather or Direct Chat Web Search connect on first launch? 100% Offline Privacy Mode is ON by default to protect your privacy. During Guided Setup (Step 1)—or anytime in Settings → Privacy, Security & Legal—toggle Online Mode ON if you want live weather forecasts, web search, or model downloads.
  • How are the Home Launcher pages arranged? Swipe horizontally across 4 pages: Direct Chat (left) · Home (center default) · Media (right 1) · HUD Gallery (right 2).
  • How does Direct LLM Chat handle web searches and the Knowledge Bank? Direct Chat uses a fast regex search-intent classifier (recognizing commands like “search the web for…” or “look up…”), checks your offline knowledge_bank.json first, reads memory.csv read-only for context, and—when Online Mode is enabled—fetches web snippets cleanly without getting stuck in an offline uncertainty loop, auto-saving PII-filtered facts to your Knowledge Bank.
  • How does the HUD Gallery handle videos, PiP, and forgotten Private PINs? Videos never autoplay while browsing or opening and stop cleanly on exit or screen lock unless you tap PiP. Drag the concentric rotary dial to step items (or scrub videos / zoom photos when maximized), or tap System Gallery to open the active file in Android’s built-in viewer. If you forget your Private Folder PIN, tap Forgot PIN? Use System Lockscreen PIN to verify via Android’s native device lockscreen and reset access.
  • What does “UK GDPR Erase All Data” remove? It permanently clears memory.csv, Cerebellum habits, knowledge_bank.json, custom plugins, Direct Chat threads, Private Gallery Vault media & PIN, Voice Vault recordings, notes, alarms, reminders, custom wallpaper, Hugging Face token, captured notifications, and enrolled face profiles from the device.
  • Where do I switch between Marcus ASCII & Circular Sci-Fi Core? Open Settings → Assistant & Voice → Emoticon Customisation (also selectable in Guided Setup).
  • Why does an AI model show “Blocked / Ultra Eco Intent Mode”? Lower-RAM or 32-bit devices or models running under the 8 tokens/second floor automatically protect device stability by keeping you on instant zero-RAM Ultra Eco intent routing.
  • How do I set Marcus as my default Home screen? Open Settings → Apps & Support → Set Marcus as Home Launcher and choose Marcus in Android’s Default Home App menu.
  • How do I rearrange Home screen widgets or Drawer apps? Long-press empty space on Home (or tap Edit Home in Settings) to reorder Home widgets and drag Marcus/system apps inside the App Drawer.
