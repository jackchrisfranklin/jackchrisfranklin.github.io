Frequently Asked Questions

My custom ROM lacks Google Speech Services. How do I use voice commands?

Open Settings → Assistant & Voice → Offline Speech Recognition Backup (Custom ROMs). You can tap Download Offline STT (~95 MB) (requires Online Mode enabled) to fetch the quantized Wav2Vec2-Base-960h ONNX backup manually, or import model_quantized.onnx and vocab.json directly from device storage. Nothing downloads automatically without your tap, and once installed Marcus automatically falls back to offline speech recognition whenever Android’s built-in speech recognizer is unavailable or errors out.

How does voice routing decide between an Android app and Marcus’s internal applets?

Commands like “open Keep Notes”, “open Gallery”, or “open Calculator” prioritise your installed Android apps first (launching Google Keep, your phone’s Gallery, etc.). If you want Marcus’s own built-in view instead, include Marcus (or your custom assistant name) or “built-in”—for example, “open Marcus notes” or “open Marcus gallery”.

Why don’t live Weather or Direct Chat Web Search connect immediately after install?

100% Offline Privacy Mode is ON by default so no background network calls leave your device without permission. In Guided Setup (Step 1)—or anytime under Settings → Privacy, Security & Legal—switch Online Mode ON if you want live forecasts, web search inside Direct Chat, or model downloads.

How do I customise Home icons, labels, and geometric backgrounds?

Open Settings → Wallpaper, Display & Home. Home shortcuts and the Bottom Dock default to borderless squircle icons with text labels hidden for a clean look. You can adjust icon size together using the Small, Medium, Large, or XL chips, toggle icon text labels on, and choose between Dark Grid (default) and five other free geometric backdrop patterns.

How do I recover access if I forget my HUD Gallery Private Folder PIN?

On the Private Folder lock screen, tap Forgot PIN? Use System Lockscreen PIN. Android’s native KeyguardManager prompt verifies your phone’s own PIN, pattern, or fingerprint and unlocks reset access without exposing your system credential to Marcus.

How are the launcher pages organised?

Swipe horizontally across four pages: Direct Chat (left) · Home (centre default) · Media (right 1) · HUD Gallery (right 2). You can hide or reorder pages in Settings → Wallpaper, Display & Home.

Why does an on-device LLM show “Blocked / Ultra Eco Intent Mode”?

To avoid out-of-memory crashes or heavy battery drain on lower-RAM or 32-bit hardware, models that exceed safe device memory or drop below the 8 tokens/second floor automatically keep your phone on zero-RAM Ultra Eco intent routing.
