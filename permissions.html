Android Permissions Disclosure (v0.2.39)

Exact permission list matching AndroidManifest.xml in Build 59 (com.jchristopherfranklin.marcus).

Permissions Intentionally Omitted for Privacy

Marcus AI: Home Launcher strictly avoids SMS inbox permissions and background ad/location tracking:

  • READ_SMS & RECEIVE_SMS — Not declared (optional notification listener covers allowlisted alerts in memory).
  • ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION — Not declared (weather GPS only runs when explicitly enabled while using the app).
  • BLUETOOTH_SCAN carries android:usesPermissionFlags="neverForLocation" — never used for location inference.
