Privacy Policy

App: Marcus AI: Home Launcher (internal codename Marcus-2, v0.2.35 / Build 55)
Android Package: com.jchristopherfranklin.marcus
Developer & Data Controller: Jack Chris Franklin (jackchrisfranklin@gmail.com)
Effective Date: November 2026

Local-First AI · Zero Ads · Zero Behavioural Trackers

1. Core Privacy Architecture

Marcus AI: Home Launcher is an Android home-screen replacement (CATEGORY_HOME) and on-device voice assistant built around local, private computation. By default, 100% Offline Privacy Mode is ON on initial launch, blocking outbound background requests for weather, web searches, and model downloads unless you disable Offline Privacy Mode or initiate an online action yourself.

  • No Developer Cloud Backend: We do not operate proprietary servers to collect, store, profile, or sell your personal conversations, notes, contacts, location, or media.
  • No Advertising or Cross-App Tracking: The app contains zero advertising SDKs, zero behavioral ad networks, and zero data brokerage.
  • No Mandatory Account Sign-Up: You do not register an email address or password to use the launcher.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

Your personal launcher configuration and assistant state stay inside your phone’s private internal storage and SharedPreferences:

  • Assistant & Theme Preferences: Configured assistant name, user honorific/title, interface theme preset, optional custom home/lock wallpaper image (custom_wallpaper.jpg), 40×19 ASCII/pixel emoticon layout, Piper-TTS voice selection, and low-battery alert rules.
  • Notes, Alarms, Timers & Reminders: Offline notes and schedule records created inside the launcher.
  • Conversation Logs (memory.csv / sync_memory.csv) & Habit Sequences: Optional on-device conversation memory and Markov habit transitions (Cerebellum). You can pause logging, edit or delete entries, export memory.csv, or wipe everything at once in Settings → Privacy, Security & Legal or the Marcus Hub.
  • Optional Face Recognition Security: Disabled by default. If you choose to enroll a face profile using the front camera, only local mathematical facial geometry vectors are stored in private on-device storage—no raw photos or biometric vectors leave your device.
  • Local Audio Player & Playlists: Indexes local audio files via Android MediaStore.Audio or folders you explicitly grant via Android’s Storage Access Framework (READ_MEDIA_AUDIO). Photos and videos are not scanned.
  • On-Device AI Models & Voices: Downloaded LiteRT-LM language models (e.g. Gemma / Qwen) and Piper-TTS ONNX voice models are stored locally in filesDir/models and filesDir/piper_voices.

3. Zero-PII Knowledge Bank & User Plugins

When enabled, Marcus caches general non-personal factual summaries in an on-device file (knowledge_bank.json) for offline answers. Users may also write or import local extensions (JavaScript, TypeScript, Python, or JSON) under Settings → Plugins & Extensions; zero third-party plugins are pre-installed.

  • Dual-Layer PII Filtering: Candidate Knowledge Bank entries are stripped of URLs and @handles and screened by regex filters (blocking emails, phone numbers, street addresses, postcodes, IPv4 addresses, API tokens, 6+ digit numbers, your configured name, and personal phrases) plus the local on-device LLM Privacy Auditor when loaded.
  • One-Tap Removal: You can disable Knowledge Bank caching, delete individual items, export JSON, or wipe all Knowledge Bank and plugin data at any time.

4. Permissions & Optional System Integrations

  • Notification Listener Access (Optional): When enabled in Android settings, MarcusNotificationListener reads active notifications locally in memory to display external media playback controls, summarize recent alerts on command, announce incoming calls, and—for Marcus Pro subscribers who enable it—prompt missed-call callback replies or read messages strictly from contacts on your Auto-Read Contacts allowlist.
  • Contacts (READ_CONTACTS, Optional): Used locally to match caller/sender names for spoken alerts. The app intentionally omits READ_SMS and RECEIVE_SMS permissions.
  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MICROPHONE): Used only when you activate voice listening, wake-word mode, or the optional Voice Notes Recorder.
  • Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION, Optional Pro Feature): Disabled by default (manual city lookup is used instead). Coordinates are used solely for local weather queries when Automatic GPS Location Updates are enabled by you while Offline Privacy Mode is off.
  • Installed Apps & Wallpaper (QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES, REQUEST_DELETE_PACKAGES, SET_WALLPAPER): Required to list, launch, hide, or uninstall apps in your Home Launcher drawer and sync launcher backgrounds to your home/lock screen.
  • Bluetooth (BLUETOOTH_CONNECT, BLUETOOTH_SCAN with neverForLocation): Used only for user-initiated hands-free Bluetooth audio device connection; never used for location tracking.

5. Subscriptions, In-App Purchases & RevenueCat

Marcus AI: Home Launcher is free to install with core launcher and on-device AI features. Optional Marcus Pro upgrades (Yearly subscription, Monthly subscription, and Lifetime unlock, entitlement marcus_ai_home_launcher_pro) are processed through Google Play Billing and verified via RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.).

  • Data Processed for Billing: When you purchase, restore purchases, or open Customer Center, Google Play and RevenueCat process an anonymous/app-generated RevenueCat App User ID, Google Play purchase tokens/receipts, product identifiers, and entitlement expiration/renewal status solely to unlock and manage your Pro subscription.
  • Offline Entitlement Cache: Your active entitlement status is cached locally on-device (SharedPreferences) so paying subscribers and lifetime owners retain Pro features when offline or in airplane mode.
  • Payment Details: Card numbers and billing addresses are handled exclusively by Google Play; the developer never sees or stores your payment card details. See RevenueCat’s Privacy Policy and Google’s Privacy Policy for their billing disclosures.

6. Optional Outbound Network Connections

When Offline Privacy Mode is turned OFF (or when you initiate a purchase/download), outbound connections occur strictly for:

  • Weather & Hourly Refinement: Open-Meteo / wttr.in requests for your chosen city or optional GPS coordinates.
  • Model & Voice Downloads: Downloading LiteRT-LM models or Piper ONNX voices from Hugging Face at your explicit request.
  • Default Browser Search: Handing off search queries to your device’s default browser (ACTION_VIEW).
  • Google Play & RevenueCat Entitlement Sync: Fetching offering prices, verifying purchases, restoring entitlements, or loading Customer Center.
  • Optional Anonymous Bug Reports: Triggered only if you tap Send Anonymous Bug Report, opening your own email client pre-addressed to jackchrisfranklin@gmail.com with paths, emails, URLs, IP addresses, phone numbers, and tokens automatically scrubbed.

7. UK GDPR Rights & Data Deletion

Because nearly all user data lives locally on your device, you exercise immediate UK GDPR control inside the app:

  • Access & Portability: Export your conversation log (memory.csv), intents (intents.json), and Knowledge Bank (knowledge_bank.json) from Settings.
  • Rectification & Erasure: Edit or delete individual memories, or tap Erase All Data in Settings → Privacy, Security & Legal to permanently clear memory.csv, Cerebellum habits, Knowledge Bank, user plugins, captured messages, and enrolled face profiles. Uninstalling the app removes all local app storage.
  • Billing Data Inquiries: To request deletion of anonymous RevenueCat purchase records associated with your App User ID, contact jackchrisfranklin@gmail.com.

Marcus AI: Home Launcher is a general-utility launcher not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable minimum digital consent age in your region). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Material changes to this policy will be posted at this URL and reflected in app updates.
