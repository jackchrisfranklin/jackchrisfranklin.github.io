Quick Answers for Beta Testers

  • Where did AI, Wallpaper, Battery Alerts & Search settings go? Everything is unified inside the single Settings applet with Android-style categories (Assistant & Voice, On-Device AI (Gemma 4), Wallpaper, Display & Home, Battery, Weather & Search, Calls, Messages & Volume, Privacy, Security & Legal, Apps & Support).
  • Why does a Gemma 4 model show “Blocked / Ultra Eco Intent Mode”? Lower-RAM or 32-bit devices or models running under the 8 tokens/second floor automatically protect device stability by keeping you on instant zero-RAM Ultra Eco intent routing.
  • How do I set Marcus as my default Home screen? Open Settings → Apps & Support → Set Marcus as Home Launcher and choose Marcus in Android’s Default Home App menu.
  • How do I rearrange Home screen widgets or Drawer apps? Long-press empty space on Home (or tap Edit Home in Settings) to reorder Home widgets and drag Marcus/system apps inside the App Drawer.
