Support & Troubleshooting

Marcus AI: Home Launcher v0.2.39 (Marcus-2, Build 59) · Developer: Jack Chris Franklin

Direct Support Contact

Need help, want to share feedback, report a crash, or inquire about a Marcus Pro purchase? Email jackchrisfranklin@gmail.com with your Android device model and OS version, or tap Send Anonymous Bug Report inside Settings → Apps & Support.

Quick Answers & Launcher Navigation

  • How are the Home Launcher pages arranged? Swipe horizontally across 4 pages: Direct Chat (left) · Home (center default) · Media (right) · Gallery (far right).
  • How does Direct LLM Chat differ from Home voice/text? Direct Chat talks directly to your offline LiteRT-LM model without TTS speech or modifying Marcus’s intent/neuromodulator state, reads memory.csv read-only for context, supports microphone speech-to-text dictation, and optionally auto-searches the web only when local model weights cannot answer (and Offline Privacy Mode allows it).
  • How does the HUD Gallery handle videos and Private items? Videos never autoplay while browsing or opening. Drag the concentric rotary dial to step items (or scrub video timelines / zoom photos when maximized), tap the expand icon or long-press the filmstrip to scan, move sensitive items to the PIN/face-locked Private Folder, or tap System to open the active file directly in Android’s built-in gallery.
  • Where do I switch between Marcus ASCII & Circular Sci-Fi Core? Open Settings → Assistant & Voice → Emoticon Customisation (also selectable in Guided Setup).
  • Why does an AI model show “Blocked / Ultra Eco Intent Mode”? Lower-RAM or 32-bit devices or models running under the 8 tokens/second floor automatically protect device stability by keeping you on instant zero-RAM Ultra Eco intent routing.
  • How do I set Marcus as my default Home screen? Open Settings → Apps & Support → Set Marcus as Home Launcher and choose Marcus in Android’s Default Home App menu.
  • How do I rearrange Home screen widgets or Drawer apps? Long-press empty space on Home (or tap Edit Home in Settings) to reorder Home widgets and drag Marcus/system apps inside the App Drawer.
